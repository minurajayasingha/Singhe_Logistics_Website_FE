Warehouse Management

Singhe Logistics' warehouse management service covers secure storage, inventory control, and efficient handling of goods for businesses across Sri Lanka. Operating from our Maharagama facility, we combine organized storage systems with careful handling procedures to keep your inventory accurate and accessible.

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified provider, we follow documented quality processes at every stage of storage and handling — from receiving and put-away to picking, packing, and dispatch — so your goods move through our facility with minimal handling risk and full accountability.

  • Organized storage systems designed for easy inventory tracking and retrieval

  • Careful receiving, put-away, and dispatch handling to minimize damage

  • Inventory accuracy maintained through consistent stock control procedures

  • Flexible storage arrangements to suit different goods and volumes

Secure Storage

Our Maharagama facility is organized to keep your goods safe, accessible, and accounted for at every stage of storage.

Reliable Handling

Every item that moves through our warehouse is handled under our ISO 9001:2015 certified quality process, from intake to dispatch.

What types of goods can you store?

We handle general cargo and goods across a range of industries at our Maharagama facility, with storage arrangements suited to different volumes and handling requirements.

How do you track inventory?

We follow consistent stock control procedures throughout receiving, storage, and dispatch to keep inventory records accurate and goods easy to locate.

Is your warehouse facility certified?

Yes, our warehouse operations are covered under our ISO 9001:2015 certification, meaning storage and handling follow documented quality processes.

Can I arrange short-term storage?

Yes, we offer flexible storage arrangements based on your volume and duration needs — get in touch to discuss your requirements.

How do I get a quote for warehousing?

Contact our team by phone or through our contact page, and we'll discuss your storage needs and provide a tailored quote.
